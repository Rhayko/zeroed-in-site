01 / Where to look

South PM warrants investigation

It handles 23.9% of due orders but 33.0% of late orders. Its 89.7% on-time rate is the lowest of the four site/shift groups. That makes it a sensible starting point for a diagnostic pilot.

02 / Metric tradeoff

Throughput does not tell the whole story

From July to December, units per labor hour rises from 15.4 to 18.0, while on-time dispatch falls from 94.0% to 91.0%. Demand and product mix change too. A productivity-only scorecard would miss the service decline.

03 / Interpret carefully

Capacity is a hypothesis to test

Capacity is assigned to 41.5% of late orders. Above-capacity shifts show 88.3% on-time dispatch versus 95.1% for other shifts. The generator encodes this relationship; it is not proof that extra staffing causes better service.

A defined business question

Where should the fictional operation focus a dispatch-reliability pilot while protecting quality and controlling overtime? The analysis compares site/shift groups, monthly weighted KPIs and recorded delay categories. It avoids ranking individual workers or converting associations into claimed savings.

Method

  1. Generate a reproducible six-month shift ledger using a fixed random seed and explicit demand, staffing, complexity and downtime assumptions.
  2. Validate unique keys, complete date/site/shift coverage, nonnegative measures, subset counts and reconciliation of order and delay totals.
  3. Calculate ratios of summed numerators and denominators using Excel SUMIFS. Review site/shift totals through a native PivotTable.
  4. Compare service, quality and labor together. Separate observed simulation results from recommendations and untested causal explanations.

The proposed next decision

Begin with a South PM diagnostic: validate standard handling times, inspect a sample of delay classifications, and identify overloaded work packets before release. Then test earlier work release, stock checks and cross-trained coverage ahead of cutoff.

A real pilot would need four weeks of baseline and four weeks of trial data, fixed cutoff definitions, simple/complex order strata and a contextual comparison shift. A provisional rule is at least a two-percentage-point service improvement without more than 0.5 points of additional rework or two points of additional overtime share. These are proposed rules; no pilot has been conducted.

What this dataset cannot establish

Daily cohorts are fully dispatched that day. Late means after the promised cutoff, not delivery to a customer. Open backlog, cancellations, returns and cross-day carryover are excluded. All weekdays operate, including holidays. Standard handling times and KPI targets are illustrative.

The simulation deliberately increases demand during November/December and reduces South PM regular hours during September/October. It uses workload to influence late probability. These choices make the dataset useful for demonstrating analysis, but they prevent treating its patterns as external evidence or a causal estimate.

Files and validation

The package includes the Excel dashboard, source CSV, field dictionary, generation script, independent validation script, summary and case-study notes.

Dashboard formulas were checked against independent totals, filtered populations, an unmatched filter and a source-cell change. All workbook tabs were visually reviewed. The downloadable workbook was also opened and refreshed successfully in desktop Excel.
